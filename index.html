01 · Trigonometry

The Unit Circle & Sine Waves

A point moves around a circle with radius 1. Its height is sin θ and its horizontal position is cos θ. Trace the height over time and you get a sine wave.

Challenge: At which two angles is sin θ = cos θ? Pause and drag to find them.

02 · Geometry

Pythagorean Theorem

In a right triangle, the square on the longest side equals the sum of the squares on the other two sides: a² + b² = c². Drag the yellow corners to reshape the triangle and watch the areas.

Challenge: Find another triangle with whole-number sides. (Hint: try a = 6.)

03 · Algebra

Parabola Explorer

Every quadratic y = ax² + bx + c draws a parabola. Move the sliders to see how each number changes the shape, the vertex, and the roots (where the curve meets the x-axis).

Challenge: Make a parabola that touches the x-axis at exactly one point. What is b² − 4ac then?
